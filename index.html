Welcome to Aura — Your Storytelling Companion ✨

A simple guide to unlocking your creative writing sanctuary.

1. Notebooks & Multi-Chapter Chronicles

Every story starts as a Notebook in your dashboard. You can add as many chapters as your imagination demands using the "+ Add Another Chapter" button. Each chapter has dedicated autosave and word counts.

2. The 9 AI Storytelling Modes

In the right-hand studio pane, toggle between modes like Continue My Story, Find Errors, Improve Writing, or Build a World. Highlight any sentence in your editor to ask Aura for direct line edits!

3. One-Click "Insert into Story"

When Aura proposes a lyrical rewrite or dialogue continuation, click the "✨ Insert into Story" button below the message to automatically merge it into your manuscript.

4. Password Protection & Cryptographic Privacy

Lock sensitive notebooks with an optional password. Passwords are hashed with SHA-256 and never stored in plain text.
